About ApexLink

A transport partner built around clarity and accountability.

Our intended approach is simple: understand the cargo, agree on the plan, communicate clearly and follow through on commitments.

Our purpose

To help businesses move goods with a more organised, transparent and responsive transport experience.

Our working principles

  • Reliability: make realistic commitments and manage changes responsibly.
  • Communication: share clear information and timely updates.
  • Safety: respect cargo handling, vehicle and site requirements.
  • Accountability: make ownership and next steps clear.
  • Professionalism: treat customers, partners and cargo with respect.
