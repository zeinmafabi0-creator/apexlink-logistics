APEXLINK LOGISTICS — WEBSITE PROTOTYPE

Included pages: Home, Services, Routes, Fleet, About, Contact / Quote Request.

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser. No build step or server is needed for local preview.
3. Navigate with the menu. On phones, tap Menu to open navigation.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- ApexLink Logistics is a placeholder brand, not a claim about a real registered business.
- Replace the demo phone +256 700 000 000, email hello@apexlinklogistics.com, and domain references.
- Confirm company name, address, operating licences, insurance, fleet, capacities, route coverage, and all claims.
- Route destinations shown are example corridor pages only and are not confirmation of service availability.
- Quote form is a front-end prototype. It validates fields and prepares a WhatsApp message, but does not save leads, email submissions, or connect to a CRM. Update the phone number in script.js and footer links before testing.
- Add a real backend/CRM or form provider before accepting live enquiries; publish a privacy notice and protect any uploaded or customer data.
- Add analytics and consent handling only after choosing the relevant tools and reviewing local privacy requirements.
- Use genuine photos with permission and add real testimonials/case studies only with customer approval.
