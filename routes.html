Cross-border readiness

Plan beyond the distance.

Cross-border freight may involve customs documentation, cargo-specific permits, border processes, insurance conditions and delivery-site restrictions. Requirements vary by shipment.

  • Confirm the exact origin and destination.
  • Identify who is responsible for export/import documentation.
  • Share cargo classification and any special requirements.
  • Agree on scope, timing and charges before dispatch.
